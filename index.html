About

Small contributions can make a meaningful difference.

Many people in Iran are struggling with the cost of basic necessities. The purpose of this initiative is simple: to collect financial support from people who want to help and direct it toward individuals and families experiencing genuine financial hardship.

Assistance is intended for essential needs such as food, medication, healthcare expenses, housing and other urgent living costs.

How it works

A simple process with accountability built in.

01

Contributions

Supporters contribute through approved external payment services. This website does not store payment-card or banking information.

02

Review

Requests for assistance are reviewed before support is provided, with priority given to genuine and urgent needs.

03

Distribution

Available funds are allocated toward essential living expenses through legally permitted channels.

04

Reporting

We publish anonymised information about collected and distributed funds while protecting recipient privacy.

Verification

How requests are reviewed.

Cases are assessed using a combination of available documentation, trusted referrals, local verification and, where possible, direct contact.

Verification cannot eliminate every uncertainty. Our aim is to make reasonable checks before assistance is provided and to document distributions internally without exposing recipients publicly.

What should I write with my contribution?

If the payment service allows you to add a note, please write Family Support Fund for the monthly family fund or Ongoing Support if you want your contribution directed toward a continuing individual case.

If no purpose is specified, the contribution will be allocated to the current campaign while it remains open.

This is an independent private aid initiative and not a registered charitable organisation. Contributions are voluntary and are not tax-deductible in Germany.

PayPal and Revolut process payments externally. This website does not collect or store card numbers or online-banking credentials.

About the initiative

A small volunteer network connecting people who can help with people who need it.

This initiative is run by a small group of Iranians inside and outside Iran. It began informally, through trusted personal networks, before this website was created.

We are not currently a registered charity. Our aim is to keep the structure simple while improving accountability, documentation and transparency as the initiative develops.

Recipient protection

Transparency does not require exposing the people receiving help.

Some recipients may be financially, medically or politically vulnerable. For that reason, identifying information is kept private.

What we may publish

  • Total amounts raised
  • Total amounts distributed
  • Number of households or individuals supported
  • General categories of assistance
  • Aggregate information about verification

What we do not publish

  • Names of recipients
  • Addresses or precise locations
  • Bank or card details
  • Medical documents
  • Names of local contacts or referrers
  • Information that could identify protest-related cases
