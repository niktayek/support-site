Who we are

A volunteer network built through personal connections.

We are a small network of Iranians living abroad, together with trusted local contacts who help us understand and verify requests for assistance.

The initiative began through personal networks and has been operating privately since January 2026.

For security reasons, we do not identify recipients or local contacts publicly. Members of the initiative based outside Iran coordinate fundraising and maintain financial records.

How it works

A simple process with accountability built in.

01

Payment notes

If you want your contribution to support a specific project, you can write Family Support Fund for the current monthly family fund, or Ongoing Support for the separate household requiring continuing monthly assistance.

Please do not include recipient names or other sensitive personal information in payment notes.

If no purpose is specified, the contribution will be allocated to the current campaign while it remains open.

This is an independent private aid initiative and not a registered charitable organisation. Contributions are voluntary.

PayPal and Revolut process payments externally. This website does not collect or store card numbers or online-banking credentials.

Recipient protection

Transparency does not require exposing the people receiving help.

Some recipients may be financially, medically or politically vulnerable. For that reason, identifying information is kept private.

What we may publish

  • Total amounts raised
  • Total amounts distributed
  • Number of households or individuals supported
  • General categories of assistance
  • Aggregate information about verification

What we do not publish

  • Names of recipients
  • Addresses or precise locations
  • Bank or card details
  • Medical documents
  • Names of local contacts or referrers
  • Information that could identify protest-related cases

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

How are contributions handled?

Contributions are collected outside Iran. Because financial transfers involving Iran are subject to significant restrictions, we do not publish operational transfer details. We take applicable financial restrictions seriously and do not knowingly provide funds to sanctioned individuals or entities.

How are families verified?

Requests are reviewed through available documentation, trusted referrals, local verification and direct communication where possible.

Why are recipient names not published?

Some recipients may face privacy or security risks. We publish aggregate information while protecting personal identities.

Important information
