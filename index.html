About

Small contributions can make a meaningful difference.

Many people in Iran are struggling with the cost of basic necessities. The purpose of this initiative is simple: to collect financial support from people who want to help and direct it toward individuals and families experiencing genuine financial hardship.

Assistance is intended for essential needs such as food, medication, healthcare expenses, housing and other urgent living costs.

How it works

A simple process with accountability built in.

01

Contributions

Supporters contribute through approved external payment services. This website does not store payment-card or banking information.

02

Review

Requests for assistance are reviewed before support is provided, with priority given to genuine and urgent needs.

03

Distribution

Available funds are allocated toward essential living expenses through legally permitted channels.

04

Reporting

We publish anonymised information about collected and distributed funds while protecting recipient privacy.
