Privacy

This website is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

Website visitors

The website does not currently require accounts, logins or user profiles.

Payments

Payments are not processed directly by this website. If payment options are activated, visitors will be redirected to an external payment provider.

This website does not store payment-card or online-banking credentials.

Recipients of assistance

Recipient records are not published on this website. Names, addresses, financial details, medical documents and other identifying information are kept outside the public website.

Email updates

No newsletter or email subscription service is currently active. If one is introduced later, this privacy notice will be updated before email addresses are collected.

Analytics and cookies

We do not currently use advertising trackers or third-party analytics tools on this website.

Contact

A dedicated contact address and the responsible person's information will be added before the public launch.
